Liber8me Ltd – Privacy Policy

Liber8me Ltd respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

01

Information we collect

When you register for a webinar, workshop or other event, or interact with our services, we may collect personal information including your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Information you choose to provide through our forms or communications
02

How we use your information

We may use your personal information to:

  • Register you for webinars, workshops and events
  • Send registration confirmations, access details, reminders and event updates
  • Deliver information, resources or services you have requested
  • Communicate with you by email
  • Where you have specifically opted in, communicate with you by SMS
  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve our services and communications
  • Send marketing communications where we have the appropriate consent or are otherwise permitted to do so
03

Email communications

When you register for a webinar, workshop or event, we may send you emails relating to your registration, including confirmations, access information, reminders, event updates and relevant follow-up information.

Where permitted, we may also send you information about our services, resources, events and offers that we believe may be relevant to you.

Marketing emails will include an unsubscribe option. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

04

SMS communications

If you choose to opt in to SMS communications, Liber8me Ltd may send you text messages relating to the webinar or event for which you registered, including registration confirmations, access information, reminders and event updates.

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of registering for the webinar or purchasing any goods or services.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of SMS messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

05

How we share information

We may share personal information with trusted service providers that help us operate our business, websites, registration systems, communications and services.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

SMS opt-in information and consent will not be sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

06

Data security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure or alteration.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or as required by applicable law.

07

Your rights

Depending on the laws applicable to you, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access to or correction of information we hold about you.

You can also unsubscribe from marketing emails or opt out of SMS communications at any time.
